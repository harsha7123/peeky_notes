Three ways to stay on track.

Peeky lives quietly at the edge of your screen until the moment it matters.

Notes that peek.

A slim handle sits on the edge of your screen. Hover it and your notes slide out. Write “call mom at 5pm” and Peeky understands.

Your routine, from a Sheet.

Already plan your day in Google Sheets? Connect it once. Every row with a time becomes a daily reminder, and edits sync automatically.

A buddy that shows up.

No easy-to-miss pop-ups. Your buddy walks in, says what to do, follows your cursor, and nudges you again if you ignore it.

Set up in a minute.

Create your account.

Sign up on the web. Your notes, routine and buddies sync everywhere.

Install on your laptop.

Open the Peeky app and click Sign in. Approve it in your browser — done.

Add notes or a Sheet.

Type to-dos with a time, or paste your Google Sheet link. Your buddy handles the rest.
